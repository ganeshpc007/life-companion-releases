# Life Companion Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026 · Applies to: Life Companion for macOS and Windows (beta)

Life Companion is a reminder app that keeps your reminders on your own computer. This policy explains, in plain words, what the app does with information. It is published by **Ganesh P C** (India), referred to here as "we".

## The short version

- Your reminders, notes, schedules and settings **stay on your computer**. There is no account and nothing is synced.
- The app checks **GitHub** for updates.
- If you agree, the app sends **error reports** with technical details only, **never your reminders' text**. You can switch this off at any time.
- No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no selling of data.

## 1. What stays on your computer

Everything you put into Life Companion:
- reminder titles, notes, categories and schedules;
- whether each reminder is on, paused, snoozed or done;
- your settings (sounds, snooze length, Do Not Disturb hints, Open at Login, error reports on or off).

These are stored in a database in the app's data folder. The app also keeps technical log files on your computer for 7 days, to help diagnose problems; they record reminder ids, not titles or notes.

We cannot see any of this. It never leaves your computer, except the technical details in an error report you agreed to send (section 3).

## 2. Update checks

About every 2 hours, and when you choose "Check for updates", the app asks GitHub (`github.com`, run by GitHub, Inc.) whether a new version exists. If you choose to update, it downloads the new version from GitHub.

Like any website, GitHub receives your computer's IP address and basic request information when the app does this. The app sends nothing else: no account, no identifier, no reminder data. GitHub's handling of this is covered by [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## 3. Error reports (only if you agree)

After installing or updating, the app asks once whether it may send error reports. Nothing is sent before you answer yes. You can change your answer any time in **Settings → Privacy**.

**When a report is made:** when the app crashes, logs an error, stops responding, or a screen fails to load. You can also send one yourself with **Send error report now**, with an optional comment. That works even when automatic reports are off, because you asked.

**What a report contains:**
- what failed and where in the app's code;
- the app version, your operating system and its version, and your processor type (for example `x86_64`);
- a random install id, created on your computer when you switch error reports on. It is not linked to your name, email or account. It lets us count how many installs a problem affects;
- the kinds of your last 50 actions in the app (for example "settings opened", "reminder card shown"), without any content;
- the last 200 technical log lines, with personal fields removed and your home folder shortened to `~`;
- your comment, if you wrote one.

**What a report never contains:** your reminders' titles, notes or schedules, your settings' values, or your files.

**Where it goes:** to a server run by us on Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc.), in our own Cloudflare account. Only we can open the reports. Cloudflare receives your IP address when the report arrives, as with any website; we do not store it with the report. Cloudflare's handling of this is covered by [Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

**How long:** reports are deleted automatically after **90 days**.

**Why:** only to find and fix problems in Life Companion.

## 4. Notifications and the reminder card

Reminders are shown through your operating system's notifications (macOS Notification Center or Windows notifications) and the app's own on-screen card. The notification text is handled by your operating system on your computer. Life Companion does not send it anywhere.

## 5. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share information with anyone. The only other parties involved are GitHub (update checks) and Cloudflare (error reports, if you agree), as described above.

## 6. Your choices and rights

- **Error reports:** switch them on or off in Settings → Privacy.
- **Delete everything on your computer:** Settings → Privacy → **Delete all my data** erases your reminders, settings, logs, unsent reports and the install id.
- **Delete reports already sent:** they are deleted after 90 days. To have them deleted sooner, email us your install id. It is shown in Settings → Privacy, and we delete every report with that id.
- **Questions or requests:** write to us (section 9).

Depending on where you live, you may have further rights over personal data, such as to access or erase it. We will honour such requests for anything we hold, which is only the error reports described above.

## 7. Children

Life Companion is a general-purpose reminder app. It does not knowingly collect any information from children, and its error reports contain no personal content.

## 8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version comes with an app update, the release notes say so, and the date at the top changes. Material changes to error reports will ask for your consent again.
